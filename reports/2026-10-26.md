## Data-quality caveat — read before relying on this report

This session's outbound network access was severely restricted: **WebFetch failed with DNS resolution errors on every external domain** (confirmed across all six research passes run today), and direct curl through the agent proxy returned 403 policy denials for career sites, job boards, and even plain control domains like google.com. Only the server-side WebSearch tool worked. Practical effect:

- **No posting could be opened and verified directly** — every finding below is a WebSearch snippet, not a confirmed-live page. Some may already be closed/expired; a few are flagged inline where the snippet itself said so.
- **Real posting dates were almost never available** — WebSearch snippets rarely carry a reliable calendar date, and per policy, vague relative labels ("18 days ago", "30d+") were not converted into dates. As a result, **Section 2 (last 30 days, confirmed) is empty today** — not because nothing is recent, but because nothing could be confirmed recent.
- **A.CRE, SelectLeaders, and LinkedIn could only be searched via indexed snippets**, not browsed live — coverage of all three is partial, and LinkedIn in particular returned no genuine linkedin.com/jobs URLs at all (listed LinkedIn-style entries below are same-market postings surfaced on other boards, flagged as such).
- **Expiry policy (consistent with prior runs during this same outage):** a previously "open" posting is only marked expired when a source explicitly stated it's closed/unavailable. Since today's agents could not re-verify most previously-tracked URLs one by one, the ~114 postings open as of the 2026-10-07 report are left untouched (still "open") in state.json rather than mass-expired on the basis of absence from a degraded search pass. This also means some of today's 70 "NEW" postings below are very likely genuine new discoveries rather than postings that went live since 10/7 — a 19-day gap occurred between this run and the prior report (no runs recorded 10/08–10/25).
