## ⚠️ Data-quality / coverage caveats

- **Gap since last run:** the previous report was 2026-10-07 — this routine appears to have gone a full month (Oct 8 – Nov 6) without firing. `state.json` is otherwise unchanged from that date, so the "NEW since last run" list below is large (117 postings) mostly because of that gap, not a sudden hiring surge.
- **WebFetch is blocked** for essentially every external domain in this sandbox (network/DNS policy) — same long-standing issue noted in prior reports. All data below comes from **WebSearch** result snippets/third-party aggregators (SelectLeaders, Built In, career.com, Greenhouse, etc.), not live verification against each employer's own careers page. Several listings carry dates or wording that suggest they may already be filled or stale even though no explicit closure signal was found; these are kept in with their dates shown as-is rather than guessed or silently dropped, per instructions.
- **Expiry policy (unchanged from prior runs):** a posting already tracked as "open" is only flipped to "expired" when a source explicitly said so (closed/no-longer-accepting/past its stated application window/employer board showing 0 openings). Absence from a single day's WebSearch-only pass is not treated as proof a role closed — so most of the 114 previously-open postings continue to carry forward as "open" below even though they weren't individually re-confirmed today.
- **LinkedIn and A.CRE job board**: `site:` search queries against both domains returned essentially nothing hosted on those actual domains (results were unrelated aggregator pages) — likely crawl/indexing restrictions on this search backend. Coverage from these two sources is effectively zero today; SelectLeaders was the only job-board source that surfaced genuine, individually identifiable postings.
- **CREFC Miami 2026 target-list rotation:** today is day-of-year 311 → offset = 311 mod 12 = **11**. Batch covered: companies at list positions 11, 23, 35, ... (34 companies, see below). Companies not in today's batch were not searched and their prior tracked status (if any) is unchanged.
- Roles above VP (Director, SVP, Chief Underwriter, Head of, Managing Director+) and roles clearly outside CRE credit/underwriting/lending scope (e.g., pure residential mortgage underwriting, IT, brokerage-only, generic non-credit finance roles) were excluded even when a company turned up search results.
